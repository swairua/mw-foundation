**Last updated: 14 October 2026**

The Mercy Wanjau Foundation ("the Foundation", "we", "our" or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information shared through our website, mw-foundation.org, in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

## 1. Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us:

- **Contact form:** your name, email address, phone number (optional), topic and message.
- **Volunteer applications:** your name, email address, phone number, county or town (optional), skills, availability and any message.
- **Partnership enquiries:** your organisation, name, role (optional), email address, phone number (optional), area of interest and message.
- **Newsletter:** your email address.

When you submit a form we also record the page you used, the date and time, and a one-way, scrambled code derived from your internet address. We use that code only to stop automated spam; we cannot use it to identify you and we do not store your internet address itself.

**Donations:** we do not collect or store card, bank or M-PESA PIN details on this website. Payments are handled directly by Safaricom M-PESA or by our card and PayPal payment providers, under their own privacy policies. We receive the transaction details needed to record and acknowledge your gift.

## 2. How we use your information

- To respond to your enquiry, application or message
- To assess volunteer applications against programme needs
- To discuss and manage partnerships
- To send updates you have subscribed to
- To keep records required for governance, accountability and the law

We rely on your consent, which you give when you submit a form, and on our legitimate interest in running the Foundation's work. We **do not sell, rent or trade** your personal information.

## 3. Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run this website. They process data only on our instructions:

- **Supabase** stores form submissions in a secure database.
- **Resend** delivers the confirmation and notification emails sent when you use a form.
- **Vercel** hosts the website.
- **Plausible Analytics** provides cookie-free, anonymous visitor statistics.

Some of these providers store data outside Kenya. Where that happens we rely on appropriate safeguards as required by the Data Protection Act, 2019. We may also disclose information when required by law, a court order or a regulator.

## 4. How long we keep it

We keep form submissions for as long as we need them to respond and to maintain records, and for no longer than 24 months after our last contact with you unless the law requires otherwise. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

## 5. Security

Information sent through this website is encrypted in transit. Submissions are stored in a database that is not publicly accessible, and only authorised Foundation staff can view them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and review our safeguards regularly.

## 6. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy
- correct information that is inaccurate or out of date
- delete your information
- stop using your information for a particular purpose, including newsletters

To exercise any of these rights, email **info@mw-foundation.org**. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) at odpc.go.ke.

## 7. Cookies and analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not collect personal information. If we ever use analytics that set cookies, they will only load after you accept them in the notice shown on your first visit. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

## 8. Children and photographs

This website is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without a parent's or guardian's consent. Photographs of children in our programmes are published only with the consent of their school or guardians; we never publish children's names, and we blur faces where appropriate.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

## 10. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact **info@mw-foundation.org** or write to the Mercy Wanjau Foundation, Mbaruk Road, Nairobi, Kenya.

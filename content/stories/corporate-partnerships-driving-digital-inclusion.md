Digital inclusion is not a task for one organization alone. It requires collective effort, and corporate partnerships are central to this mission. The Mercy Wanjau Foundation’s Technology Pillar thrives on collaboration with corporations and educational institutions, ensuring sustainability and impact.

## The Challenge of Rural Exclusion

Corporations bring resources, expertise, and networks. Educational institutions provide venues, curricula, and mentorship. Together, they amplify the Foundation’s reach and deepen its impact.

### Examples of Collaboration

- **Tech Company Partnership**: A leading software firm donated devices and provided training for youth in Kirinyaga County.
- **University Collaboration**: A local university partnered to deliver coding workshops, integrating students into real-world projects.

## Sustainability Through Partnerships

Partnerships ensure that programs are not one-off initiatives but sustainable models. Corporate sponsors provide ongoing support, while educational institutions embed digital literacy into curricula.

Youth trained through partnerships have secured internships, launched startups, and contributed to community development. Persons with disabilities have gained remote work opportunities, breaking barriers of exclusion.

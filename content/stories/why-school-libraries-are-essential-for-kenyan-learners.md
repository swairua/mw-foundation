In many Kenyan schools, especially in rural areas, students attend classes without access to a library. This means limited reading materials, few textbooks, and no quiet place to study independently. For the Mercy Wanjau Foundation, this gap is not just an educational concern it is a social justice issue.

A library represents opportunity. It symbolizes access, imagination, knowledge, and empowerment. That is why the Foundation is passionately committed to building school libraries across Kenya, beginning with underserved communities.

## The Critical Role Libraries Play in Learning

Research consistently shows that libraries have a transformative impact on children’s development. With access to books and learning materials, students develop stronger vocabulary, better comprehension, and improved critical-thinking skills.

Libraries help students:

- Build reading habits
- Strengthen academic skills
- Improve performance in literacy and science
- Explore interests beyond the classroom
- Develop focus and creativity
- Gain independence in learning

A school library is not a luxury, it is a necessity.

## Kenya’s Literacy Challenge

Despite progress in the education sector, many Kenyan students struggle with reading proficiency by the time they reach upper primary. This challenge is often linked to:

- Lack of reading materials
- Overcrowded classrooms
- Minimal teacher-student interaction
- Limited exposure to diverse literature

Establishing libraries directly addresses these gaps by bringing books closer to the learners who need them most.

## The Foundation’s School Library Project

The Mercy Wanjau Foundation is working with willing schools to create well-equipped libraries within their compounds. Each library is designed to be:

- **Inclusive** (accommodating students with disabilities)
- **Child-friendly**
- **Resource-rich**
- **Safe and inspiring**

Libraries are stocked with:

- Storybooks
- Curriculum materials
- Reference books
- Digital devices
- Braille materials (where needed)
- Reading games and visual aids

## Impact on Communities

A library’s impact extends beyond students. Parents, teachers, and community members also benefit from having a hub of learning within reach. Some libraries serve as community reading centers after school hours, helping strengthen literacy culture across generations.

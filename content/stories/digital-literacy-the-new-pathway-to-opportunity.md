In the 21st century, digital literacy is as essential as reading and writing. Without it, individuals are excluded from education, employment, and civic participation. The Mercy Wanjau Foundation’s Technology Pillar recognizes digital literacy as the new pathway to opportunity.

Digital literacy goes beyond knowing how to use a computer. It includes navigating the internet safely, using productivity tools, coding, and understanding digital ethics. These skills empower individuals to access information, communicate effectively, and innovate. They are the foundation of participation in the digital economy.

The Foundation’s training programs are tailored to diverse groups, including youth, women, and persons with disabilities. From basic computer skills to advanced coding, we provide inclusive opportunities for learning. Our tech hubs serve as safe spaces where learners can explore, experiment, and grow.

## Whats the Impact?

The impact is tangible. Students gain confidence to pursue higher education. Entrepreneurs use digital tools to expand their businesses. Communities access online resources to improve livelihoods. Digital literacy transforms lives, fostering dignity and resilience.

> Technology will not replace great teachers, but technology in the hands of great teachers can be transformational.
>
> — George Couros, author and educator

But the journey requires collective effort. Volunteers can serve as trainers and mentors. Corporate partners can provide resources and expertise. Donors can fund devices, internet access, and training materials. Each contribution helps light the way toward a digitally inclusive Kenya.

Digital literacy is not optional, it is essential. By embedding it into our programs, the Mercy Wanjau Foundation ensures that every Kenyan has the opportunity to thrive in the digital age. We stand as a beacon of hope, illuminating pathways to empowerment through knowledge and skills.

The digital divide is not insurmountable. With vision, collaboration, and commitment, we can ensure that every Kenyan has the tools to thrive in the digital age. The Mercy Wanjau Foundation stands as a beacon of hope, illuminating pathways to empowerment through technology.

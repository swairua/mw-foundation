In today’s world, access to technology is no longer a luxury, it is a necessity. Yet across Kenya, millions of people remain excluded from the digital revolution. The Mercy Wanjau Foundation recognizes that this divide is not just about devices or internet connections; it is about opportunity, dignity, and empowerment.

The digital divide manifests in many ways: rural schools without computers, youth unable to access online learning, and persons with disabilities excluded from digital platforms. Without intervention, these gaps perpetuate inequality and limit Kenya’s potential. Technology is the great equalizer, but only if it is accessible to all.

The Foundation’s Technology Pillar is designed to bridge this divide. By establishing tech hubs in underserved communities, we provide safe spaces where youth and marginalized groups can learn, innovate, and connect. These hubs are more than buildings with computers, they are gateways to opportunity. They offer training in digital literacy, coding, entrepreneurship, and online safety, equipping participants with skills that open doors to education and employment.

## Supporting Persons with Disabilities

For persons with disabilities, technology is transformative. Assistive devices such as screen readers, adaptive keyboards, and specialized software enable independence and access to remote work opportunities. By embedding inclusivity into our programs, we ensure that no one is left behind.

> A person who is severely impaired never knows his hidden sources of strength until he is treated like a normal human being and encouraged to shape his own life.
>
> — Helen Keller

But bridging the divide requires collective effort. Donors can help fund devices and internet access. Corporate partners can provide expertise and mentorship. Volunteers can share their skills, guiding learners through the digital landscape. Each contribution, no matter how small, lights the way toward a digitally inclusive Kenya.

The digital divide is not insurmountable. With vision, collaboration, and commitment, we can ensure that every Kenyan has the tools to thrive in the digital age. The Mercy Wanjau Foundation stands as a beacon of hope, illuminating pathways to empowerment through technology.

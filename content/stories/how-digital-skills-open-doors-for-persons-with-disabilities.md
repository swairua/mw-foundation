Access to digital technology can transform lives, especially for PWDs who face barriers to traditional employment. Mercy Wanjau Foundation equips PWDs with digital skills that allow them to work remotely, start online businesses, and participate in the global economy.

### 1. Digital Literacy Training Programs

Programs include:

- Computer basics and office software
- Graphic design, digital marketing, and freelancing
- Online communication and professionalism
- Adapted materials for various disabilities

This ensures PWDs gain marketable skills for local and international opportunities.

### 2. Assistive Technology and Accessibility

Assistive tools like screen readers, voice recognition, and ergonomic devices allow PWDs to perform digital tasks efficiently. MWF provides these tools and trains participants on their use.

## How You Can Help

- Donate assistive devices or software licenses
- Mentor PWDs in digital entrepreneurship
- Sponsor training sessions or digital labs
- Advocate for inclusive tech in workplaces

Every contribution helps PWDs achieve independence and financial security.

## Conclusion

Digital skills open doors to opportunity for PWDs. Mercy Wanjau Foundation equips them with tools, knowledge, and mentorship to succeed. Supporting these programs ensures PWDs can fully participate in Kenya’s economy and society.

## How Corporates Can Partner with the Foundation

### Sponsor Educational Programs

Companies can fund programs such as:

- School library construction
- The French Initiative
- Leadership and mentorship workshops
- Scholarships for vulnerable students

### Provide Learning Materials and Resources

Examples include:

- Textbooks
- Computers or tablets
- Science lab equipment
- Braille materials

### Employee Volunteer Programs

Corporates can encourage staff to volunteer as mentors, literacy coaches, or workshop facilitators.

### Long-Term Institutional Partnerships

Organizations may enter multi-year agreements to support entire counties or targeted school clusters.

## Benefits to Corporates

### Enhanced Brand Reputation

Companies seen actively supporting education are viewed as socially responsible and community-minded.

### Employee Engagement

Staff participating in volunteering programs feel more fulfilled and connected to the company’s mission.

### Positive Community Relationships

Communities develop trust and appreciation for companies that invest in their children’s futures.

### Sustainable Impact

Corporate involvement creates long-term change rather than short-term fixes.

## Conclusion

Corporate partnerships are essential to advancing education and leadership development in Kenya. When nonprofits and the private sector collaborate, their combined impact becomes powerful enough to transform communities for generations.

Mental health is essential for thriving communities. In Kenya, mental health challenges are increasingly recognized, yet stigma, lack of awareness, and limited access to professional support continue to prevent many people from seeking help. The Mercy Wanjau Foundation (MWF) Wellness pillar focuses on both physical and mental health, with an emphasis on **mental wellness education**, early intervention, and preventative support.

> There is no health without mental health.
>
> — World Health Organization

### 1. Understanding the Mental Health Landscape in Kenya

Mental health issues affect people of all ages, but awareness remains low. Many individuals suffer in silence due to societal stigma or lack of knowledge. Common issues include depression, anxiety, stress-related disorders, and trauma from personal or community experiences.

MWF’s monthly online engagement sessions provide education on recognizing mental health signs, understanding coping strategies, and knowing when to seek professional care. These sessions help communities develop a language around mental wellness and reduce fear or misunderstanding.

### 2. The Role of Online Wellness Engagement

MWF leverages online platforms to reach people where they are, making mental health support accessible even in remote areas. Participants learn:

- How to have supportive conversations
- First-level care techniques similar to physical first aid
- Strategies for self-care and stress management
- When and how to refer someone to professional services

Early feedback shows that participants gain confidence and practical tools to support themselves and others, which is encouraging for future programming.

## How You Can Help

There are many ways individuals and organizations can support wellness initiatives:

- **Fund a Wellness Cohort**: Support the creation and facilitation of online sessions for new communities.
- **Volunteer Expertise**: Mental health professionals can contribute as facilitators or mentors.
- **Develop Education Materials**: Help create culturally relevant mental health guides for schools, churches, and communities.
- **Advocate for Awareness**: Promote mental wellness conversations in local networks to reduce stigma.

Each contribution expands access, strengthens impact, and fosters resilient communities.

## Conclusion

Mental wellness is not only a personal issue but a societal one. By equipping communities with knowledge, tools, and support networks, the Mercy Wanjau Foundation empowers Kenyans to recognize, address, and prevent mental health challenges. Your involvement—whether through volunteering, funding, or advocacy—ensures that mental health becomes a priority, helping communities thrive socially, emotionally, and economically.

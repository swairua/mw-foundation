Agriculture is the backbone of Kenya’s economy, with smallholder farmers providing food security for millions. Yet, many farmers face challenges including unpredictable weather, limited access to modern farming techniques, and restricted markets. These barriers make it difficult to generate stable income or scale operations. The Mercy Wanjau Foundation (MWF) addresses these issues through economic empowerment programs tailored to farmers.

### 1. Demonstration Plots and Practical Training

MWF organizes demonstration plots where farmers learn hands-on skills in:

- Improved seed selection
- Soil fertility management
- Sustainable farming techniques
- Post-harvest handling and storage

This approach allows farmers to apply new methods immediately and observe tangible results.

### 2. Access to Inputs and Resources

Many farmers lack access to critical resources such as seeds, fertilizers, tools, and irrigation equipment. MWF facilitates access to these inputs through partnerships and sponsorship programs. Farmers who receive these resources are able to increase yield and diversify crops, reducing the risk of income loss.

### 3. Market Linkages and Income Growth

Empowerment does not stop at production. Farmers are also trained in marketing, pricing, and selling their produce efficiently. By connecting farmers with local and regional markets, MWF ensures their efforts translate into sustainable income.

## How You Can Help

- Sponsor a demonstration plot or farmer training cohort
- Donate farm inputs, tools, or seeds
- Volunteer as an agribusiness mentor
- Advocate for smallholder-friendly policies in your county

## Conclusion

Empowering farmers is crucial to Kenya’s food security and economic development. By supporting practical training, access to inputs, and market linkages, we help smallholder farmers achieve sustainable livelihoods. Every contribution strengthens the backbone of Kenya’s agricultural sector.

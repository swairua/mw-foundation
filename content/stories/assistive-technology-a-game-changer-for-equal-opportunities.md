Assistive technology helps PWDs overcome barriers to education, employment, and daily life. Mercy Wanjau Foundation provides devices, software, and training that enable PWDs to work, learn, and communicate effectively.

> Technology is best when it brings people together.
>
> — Matt Mullenweg

### Types of Assistive Technology

MWF provides:

- Screen readers for visually impaired users
- Voice recognition software
- Ergonomic keyboards and adaptive devices
- Mobility and communication aids

These tools enhance independence and productivity.

### Training and Support

Devices alone are not enough. MWF trains participants on proper use, troubleshooting, and integration into daily routines. Mentorship ensures learners can maximize technology for work and education.

## How You Can Help

- Donate assistive technology or fund devices
- Volunteer as a technology trainer
- Advocate for digital accessibility in workplaces and schools

Your support ensures more PWDs can thrive independently.

## Conclusion

Assistive technology is transformative for PWDs. By combining tools, training, and mentorship, Mercy Wanjau Foundation empowers PWDs to participate fully in society. Contributions make this impact possible.

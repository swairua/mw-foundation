Kenya’s digital economy is growing rapidly, but women remain underrepresented. Cultural barriers, limited access to resources, and societal stigma often prevent women from participating fully. The Mercy Wanjau Foundation’s Technology Pillar is committed to changing this narrative.

Women are not just beneficiaries of technology, they are drivers of innovation. When empowered with digital skills, women entrepreneurs expand businesses, mothers support children’s education, and leaders inspire communities. Inclusion of women in the digital economy is not just fair; it is essential for sustainable growth.

The Foundation’s programs target women specifically, offering training in digital literacy, entrepreneurship, and leadership. Tech hubs provide safe spaces where women can learn and collaborate. Mentorship connects them with role models who demonstrate that women can thrive in technology.

The digital divide manifests in many ways: rural schools without computers, youth unable to access online learning, and persons with disabilities excluded from digital platforms. Without intervention, these gaps perpetuate inequality and limit Kenya’s potential. Technology is the great equalizer, but only if it is accessible to all.

The Foundation’s Technology Pillar is designed to bridge this divide. By establishing tech hubs in underserved communities, we provide safe spaces where youth and marginalized groups can learn, innovate, and connect. These hubs are more than buildings with computers, they are gateways to opportunity. They offer training in digital literacy, coding, entrepreneurship, and online safety, equipping participants with skills that open doors to education and employment.

Support from donors, partners, and volunteers is vital. Sponsorship schemes can fund women’s training cohorts. Corporate partners can provide mentorship and resources. Volunteers can serve as instructors and coaches. Each contribution helps dismantle barriers and create opportunities.

Women’s participation in the digital economy is not just about equality — it is about progress. By empowering women, we empower families, communities, and the nation. The Mercy Wanjau Foundation stands as a beacon of hope, illuminating pathways to empowerment for women in Kenya’s digital future.
